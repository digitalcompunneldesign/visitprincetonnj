Where to eat

01 — Palmer Square

Nassau Inn

For more than 260 years, the historic inn in Palmer Square has been welcoming visitors from around the world. It is known for its blend of historical charm and stylish, contemporary amenities, as well as the Yankee Doodle Tap Room Restaurant.

02 — Downtown

The Peacock Inn

Located in the heart of downtown, this luxury boutique hotel features an upscale fine-dining restaurant. Built in the 1700s, the Colonial-style mansion was moved to its current location in 1875 and is today part of the Ascend Hotel Collection.
