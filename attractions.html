05 — Princeton attractions

Places to start with

Here are just some of the many historical, cultural and recreational attractions Princeton has to offer. (Sample to Discuss)

The full list

27 places to start with

Showing all 27 places

01

Art@Bainbridge

A gallery project of the Princeton University Art Museum, housed in the carefully restored colonial-era Bainbridge House on Nassau Street in downtown Princeton. Admission is free.

02

Arts Council of Princeton

A non-profit independent arts organization in Princeton, NJ, offering a range of classes, events and galleries.

03

Back Nine Golf

An indoor golf simulator in the Princeton Shopping Center.

04

Battlefield State Park

Where American and British troops fought each other on January 3, 1777, in the Battle of Princeton during the American Revolutionary War. The battle ended when the British soldiers in Nassau Hall surrendered.

05

D&R Canal

Over 70 miles of linear multi-use path along a historic canal, with trail networks and connections across several counties. Pack your hiking shoes, grab your mountain bike or strap that kayak to the car.

06

D&R Greenway

Since its founding in 1989, the D&R Greenway Trust has preserved 22,000 acres of land in New Jersey. Visitors can walk its trails and enjoy the lands they have helped protect.

07

Drumthwacket

Constructed on land that witnessed the 1777 Princeton battle for American independence, today it serves as the official residence of the Governor of New Jersey.

08

Friends of Herrontown Woods

Herrontown Woods is Princeton’s first and most whimsical nature preserve.

09

Historical Society of Princeton

Founded in 1939 and located at Updike Farmstead, it is a hub for cultural enrichment and experiential education that includes a museum and research center.

10

Hudson Table

An interactive culinary studio and event space offering cooking classes, live chef competitions, catering and more.

11

Institute Woods

Open to the public and enjoyed year-round by bird watchers, walkers, runners and cross-country skiers.

12

Lake Carnegie

Located in the northeastern portion of Princeton, a lovely reservoir built in 1906 by damming the Millstone River.

13

McCarter Theatre

McCarter’s mission is to create stories and experiences that enliven minds, expand imaginations and engage communities via the performing arts.

14

Morven Museum & Garden

A National Historic Landmark on five pristine acres — home of Richard Stockton, a signer of the Declaration of Independence, and a former Governor’s Mansion — showcasing the state’s cultural heritage through exhibitions, programs and events.

15

Palmer Square Princeton

An exceptional collection of brand name stores, one-of-a-kind boutiques, great places to dine and exciting signature events.

16

Princeton Battlefield Society

The officially recognized friends organization for the Princeton Battlefield State Park.

17

Princeton Cemetery

A historic graveyard of the Nassau Presbyterian Church that is still active today. The nondenominational burial ground is best known as the final resting place of many notable individuals.

18

Princeton Family Adventure Center

Fun, fitness and more at this indoor adventure space for children at the Princeton Shopping Center.

19

Princeton Garden Theatre

A historic movie theater on Nassau Street dedicated to presenting the finest independent, international, documentary and classic films and series.

20

Princeton Public Library

The most visited municipal public library in New Jersey, with over 860,000 annual visitors.

21

Princeton Shopping Center

Home to more than 40 local and national retail outlets.

22

Princeton University Art Museum

A world-class art collection at the heart of the Princeton University campus.

23

Princeton University Athletics

Men’s and women’s teams compete in multiple sports, from basketball to water polo.

24

Princeton University Concerts

A mission to educate, challenge, inspire and unite audiences through the presentation of exceptional classical musicians.

25

Springdale Golf Club

A 15,000 square foot clubhouse with a formal dining room, bar/grille room and a separate conference room — the dining opportunities are endless.

26

The Princeton Festival

Princeton’s premier music festival, the Princeton Symphony Orchestra’s flagship summer program.

27

TPC Jasna Polana

A private golf club in Princeton that offers fine dining and hosts business events, conferences and weddings.

A look around

Historical, cultural and recreational — all of it close together.
